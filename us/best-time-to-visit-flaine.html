G
Gilles's view
Founder · BestDateWeather

Flaine, in the Grand Massif: 5,200 ft, altitude resort (the highest in Grand Massif), Brutalist architecture by Marcel Breuer (Bauhaus architect, student of Mies van der Rohe), opened in 1969 = subject of architectural polemic (raw concrete in the heart of the Alps, considered the ugliest or the most visionary depending on opinion). August is my favorite month (8.2/10): 52-72°F, 39% rain in storms, alpine hiking. From June to September, expect 39-72°F: short summer season. From December to March, expect 21/41°F and 41-48% precipitation often as snow: ski high season, very good snow cover thanks to altitude. Pistes: 165 miles Grand Massif (Flaine + Samoëns + Morillon + Sixt + Les Carroz, 1 single ski pass), altitude 700-8,200 ft, vertical drop 5,900 ft. iconic pistes: Cascades (one of the longest green pistes in the world, 9 miles from Tête des Lindars 8,100 ft down to Sixt 2,500 ft, accessible to beginners!), Combe de Gers (shaded north face, preserved snow). Off-piste: Combe du Fer-à-Cheval, Aiguille du Plan Praz. Audience: families, school groups (Flaine welcomes many snow-class school groups thanks to UCPA residences), functional rather than smart atmosphere. Hotels: Totem (iconic Breuer architecture, Hotel Le Refuge du Golf, Hotel Aujon), $110-$440/night. Sculptures: Picasso ("Tête de femme"), Vasarely, Dubuffet exhibited outdoors in the resort. Try tartiflette, fondue. Logistics: Flaine 1h15 from Geneva (mandatory parking at the foot of the resort, pedestrian access only for cars), Magny-Cluses-Flaine shuttles from the TGV station.

How we calculate the score

For mountain destinations, we calculate two scores per month — skiing and hiking — and display the higher of the two.

⛷️Ski score DEC → MAY
  • 40% snow cover (slope T° corrected by lapse rate -3.6°F/1000ft)
  • 40% winter thermal comfort (23–41°F optimal)
  • 20% sunshine
  • Off-season penalty: ×0.70 adjacent months · ×0.35 outside season
🥾Hiking score JUN → SEP
  • 40% temperature (50–72°F optimal)
  • 40% rain (frequency + daily intensity)
  • 20% sunshine

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Flaine?
It depends on the activity. For skiing: December (ski score 8.2/10). For hiking/suiner: December (8.2/10, 3.0°F).
Can you ski in Flaine in winter?
Yes, it's peak ski season. Average ski score December–February: 8.0/10. Cold temperatures (2°F max in January) and frequent snowfall.
Is it warm in Flaine in suiner?
In December, temperatures average 3.0°F. Ideal for hiking and outdoor activities.
What is the wettest month in Flaine?
November is the wettest month with November has the lowest climate score of the year.

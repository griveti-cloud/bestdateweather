Mbabane, administrative capital of Eswatini (formerly Swaziland until renamed 2018 by King Mswati III) in southern Africa: 95,000 inhabitants at 3,900 ft altitude. Country among the smallest in Africa (6,700 sq miles), landlocked between South Africa and Mozambique. Last absolute monarchy in Africa = King Mswati III rules without constitution since 1986, controversy over personal wealth vs national poverty (HIV rate the highest in the world 27% adult population). Preserved cultural country with Swazi traditions and annual royal ceremonies. May-June-July are my favorite months (7.9-8.2/10): 46-70°F, 5-13% rain, this is the ideal dry season thanks to altitude. August-September stay excellent (8.0-8.2/10). From May to September, it's the dry season: 46-72°F, temperate African climate, clear sky for wildlife observation. From October to April, it's the wet season: 27-68% rainfall, but stable temperatures 57-77°F. Permanent climate: Mbabane never tops 77°F in maximum average and rarely descends below 46°F in minimum average thanks to its altitude 3,900 ft, ideal temperate African climate contrasting with subtropical Cape Town South Africa. Highlights: Mbabane Center (modest little touristic African capital in itself), Swazi Plaza and Swazi Market (local atmosphere, Swazi craftsmanship), Ezulwini Valley 9 miles away ("Valley of the Sky", the main tourist area of the country, former Swazi kingdom, 4-5* casino hotels, livelier atmosphere than Mbabane), Mantenga Cultural Village ("traditional Swazi cultural village", reconstruction of a traditional Swazi village with traditional dance demonstrations, reconstituted Swazi wedding, culturally informative pedagogical atmosphere), Sibebe Rock ("Bald Rock", granite monolith 2nd largest in the world after Uluru Australia, 2 miles panoramic ascent), House of Fire (cultural-gallery center with annual festivals), Big Bend and Lobamba (official royal and legislative capitals, royal palace Embo State Palace, parliament). Swazi royal ceremonies = the great cultural attraction of Eswatini: Umhlanga / Reed Dance (August-September, 100,000 virgin young girls present reeds to the Queen Mother and the King in millennial ceremony = unique world Swazi cultural iconic, international controversy as the king can choose a new wife from among them), Incwala / First Fruits Ceremony (December-January, sacred royal ceremony forbidden to foreigners). Essential Eswatini excursions: Hlane Royal National Park (national park, lions, elephants, rhinoceros, Big 4 except leopards), Mlilwane Wildlife Sanctuary (more accessible reserve, hiking-bike-horse safari), Mkhaya Game Reserve (black and white rhinoceros), Malolotja Nature Reserve (panoramic mountains with Malolotja Canopy Tour suspended walkway). International excursions: Kruger National Park South Africa 2h away, Maputo Mozambique 2h away. Try sishwala (corn porridge), siphuphe (vegetables), nyama yenkomo (grilled beef braai), umngqusho (corn beans), imported South African wines, local Sibebe Beer. King Mswati III airport at Manzini (SHO).

How we calculate the score

For mountain destinations, we calculate two scores per month — skiing and hiking — and display the higher of the two.

⛷️Ski score DEC → MAY
  • 40% snow cover (slope T° corrected by lapse rate -3.6°F/1000ft)
  • 40% winter thermal comfort (23–41°F optimal)
  • 20% sunshine
  • Off-season penalty: ×0.70 adjacent months · ×0.35 outside season
🥾Hiking score JUN → SEP
  • 40% temperature (50–72°F optimal)
  • 40% rain (frequency + daily intensity)
  • 20% sunshine

Questions

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Mbabane?
It depends on the activity. For skiing: June (ski score 2.1/10). For hiking/suiner: July (9.1/10, 20.0°F).
Can you ski in Mbabane in winter?
Yes, it's peak ski season. Average ski score December–February: 1.7/10. Cold temperatures (24°F max in January) and frequent snowfall.
Is it warm in Mbabane in suiner?
In July, temperatures average 20.0°F. Ideal for hiking and outdoor activities.
What is the wettest month in Mbabane?
December is the wettest month with December has the lowest climate score of the year.

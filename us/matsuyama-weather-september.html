What to expect in September

Autumn in Matsuyama is often underrated: 27.0°F, golden light and declining crowds. An interesting window. Expect rain on 42.0% of days — a light raincoat in the bag is recommended. A good balance between weather and crowds, even if September (7.9/10) remains theoretically better.

September is the best month of the year in Matsuyama. Compared to August: −5°F daytime, −1.5h of sun per day.

Gilles's view
Founder · BestDateWeather

Saas-Fee is the Swiss "village of glaciers" in the Valais: 5,900 ft altitude, surrounded by 13 summits over 13,100 ft (Dom, Allalinhorn, Täschhorn). Car-free village (mandatory parking at the entrance). July is my favorite month (7.6/10): 48-66°F, 54% rain, good for hiking towards the Mittelallalin (11,500 ft, accessible by funicular) and the rotating Allalin restaurant. From December to April, expect 16/36°F: ski season. Permanent summer skiing on the Fee glacier (one of the rare in the world). From November and May, it's the shoulder season. Traditional chalet architecture, unlike the neighbouring concrete French resorts. Population: 1700 permanent residents. Access by train from Brig (1h by bus) or car via the St Bernard tunnel from Italy. High Swiss prices (250-500 CHF/night for a 4-star, day pass 80-90 CHF).

How we calculate the score

For mountain destinations, we calculate two scores per month — skiing and hiking — and display the higher of the two.

⛷️Ski score DEC → MAY
  • 40% snow cover (slope T° corrected by lapse rate -3.6°F/1000ft)
  • 40% winter thermal comfort (23–41°F optimal)
  • 20% sunshine
  • Off-season penalty: ×0.70 adjacent months · ×0.35 outside season
🥾Hiking score JUN → SEP
  • 40% temperature (50–72°F optimal)
  • 40% rain (frequency + daily intensity)
  • 20% sunshine

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time to visit Saas-Fee?
It depends on the activity. For skiing: April (ski score 8.6/10). For hiking/suiner: April (8.6/10, 5.0°F).
Can you ski in Saas-Fee in winter?
Yes, it's peak ski season. Average ski score December–February: 7.5/10. Cold temperatures (-1°F max in January) and frequent snowfall.
Is it warm in Saas-Fee in suiner?
In April, temperatures average 5.0°F. Ideal for hiking and outdoor activities.
What is the wettest month in Saas-Fee?
June is the wettest month with June has the lowest climate score of the year.

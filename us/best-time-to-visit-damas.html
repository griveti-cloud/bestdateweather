Gilles's view
Founder · BestDateWeather

Damascus (Dimashq), capital of Syria: 1.7 million inhabitants intra-muros, 2.7 million in urban area (before the civil war). One of the oldest continuously inhabited cities in the world = 11,000 years of continuous human occupation, mentioned in Egyptian texts 2500 BC, capital of the Umayyad Caliphate 661-750 = capital of the most extensive Islamic empire in history extending from Portugal to the Indus. May is my favorite month (9.4/10): 55-86°F, 8% rain, this is the ideal spring. April-October stay excellent (8.4-8.7/10). From April to June and September-October, these are the best tourist seasons: 48-88°F, ideal Mediterranean-desert climate. From July to August drop to 3.9/10 due to the heatwave (68-99°F, sometimes 113°F+). From November to March, expect 34-63°F and 9-21% rain: mild but cool nights, rare snow possible on the neighbouring mountains. Highlights: Old town of Damascus (UNESCO 1979, one of the oldest urban cities in the world, preserved millennial Oriental atmosphere, labyrinthine alleys, Ottoman palaces, hammams), Umayyad Mosque (Al-Jami' al-Umawi, 706-715 AD, one of the oldest mosques in the world and one of the holiest after those of Mecca-Medina-Jerusalem, houses the head of Saint John the Baptist according to tradition + Saladin's tomb in the outer garden, architectural iconic view of classical Islam with its preserved Byzantine mosaics), Saladin's Tomb (Salah ad-Din, 1193, Muslim hero who took back Jerusalem from the Crusaders 1187), Citadel of Damascus (medieval 13th-century Ayyubid), Souk Al-Hamidiyah ("Hamidiyah Souk", 19th-century traditional covered market, one of the largest souks in the Arab world, 2,000 ft long with metal sheet roofing iconic, Ottoman heritage of Sultan Abdülhamid II), Maktab Anbar House (traditional Damascene notable's palace, preserved Oriental atmosphere), Azem Palace (1750, Ottoman governor Asad Pasha al-Azm's palace, become folk arts museum), Straight Street ("Via Recta", street mentioned in the biblical Acts of the Apostles where Saint Paul converted after his vision on the road to Damascus, still present in the old town), Bab Touma Christian Quarter ("Saint Thomas Gate", one of the oldest Christian quarters in the world, preserved picturesque atmosphere). Safety: Syria coming out of civil war 2011-2024 = the fall of the Assad regime in December 2024 by the HTS forces of Ahmed al-Sharaa (Abu Mohammed al-Joulani) ended 13 years of bloody civil war (500,000+ deaths), new Sunni Islamist transitional government in place, political situation still very unstable, inadvisable France Diplomacy for the entirety of the country, tourist infrastructure destroyed or abandoned, very inadvisable travel except rare humanitarian-journalistic missions. Try Syrian mezze (world culinary iconic of the Levant), Damascus kebab, fattoush, tabbouleh, kibbeh, Syrian baklava, Arabic coffee with cardamom, mint tea. Damascus International airport (DAM).

How we calculate the score

The score combines 4 weighted criteria using 10 years of real climate data (ERA5).

  • 40% temperature (sweet spot 72-82°F, extreme heat penalty)
  • 35% rain (frequency + daily intensity)
  • 25% sunshine (hours per day)
  • Humidity penalty (dew point ≥ 61°F + tmax ≥ 79°F)

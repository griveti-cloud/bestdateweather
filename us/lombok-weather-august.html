Context

Our verdict

Lombok, Bali's neighbouring island (22 miles to the east): 1,810 sq miles, 4 million inhabitants, quieter and less touristy than Bali, beaches among the wildest in Indonesia, dominated by the Rinjani volcano (12,200 ft, Indonesia's 2nd highest). July-August are my favorite months (8.3/10): 70-84°F, 40-44% rain, this is the dry season.

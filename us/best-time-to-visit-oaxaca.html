Gilles's view
Founder · BestDateWeather

Oaxaca is the most culturally dense Mexico: Zapotec zones, artisanal mezcal, pre-Columbian markets still alive. At 4,900 ft altitude, the climate stays mild year-round. February is my favorite month (9.5/10): 52-82°F, 2% rain, clear sky for Monte Albán or Hierve el Agua. From June to September, it's the tropical rainy season (59-70% showers), often late afternoon but sometimes long. This is the greenest period, interesting for photography if you accept the uncertainty. Día de los Muertos late October-early November is the absolute tourist peak: hotel prices x3-x5, bookings 6 months ahead, but unique atmosphere worldwide (illuminated cemeteries, catrina parades). The Guelaguetza in July is another high point, despite the rain. November-May stay the stable dry season.

How we calculate the score

The score combines 4 weighted criteria using 10 years of real climate data (ERA5).

  • 40% temperature (sweet spot 72-82°F, extreme heat penalty)
  • 35% rain (frequency + daily intensity)
  • 25% sunshine (hours per day)
  • Humidity penalty (dew point ≥ 61°F + tmax ≥ 79°F)

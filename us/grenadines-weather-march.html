What to expect in March

Spring marks the start of the good season in Grenadines. Temperatures rise (26.0°F) and tourists have not yet arrived en masse. Rain risk remains low (22.0% of days), leaving good room for outdoor activities. A good balance between weather and crowds, even if April (8.7/10) remains theoretically better.

Context

Our verdict

Grenadines (Saint Vincent and the Grenadines), Caribbean micro-state in the south of the Lesser Antilles : 110,000 inhabitants on 32 islands of which 9 inhabited. The country is made up of the main island Saint Vincent + 32 small Grenadines islets (Bequia, Mustique, Canouan, Mayreau, Union Island, Tobago Cays, Palm Island, Petit Saint Vincent).
